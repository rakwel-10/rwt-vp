Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Three different things are sold through this site, and each refunds a little differently: Lounge hours, membership, and home wellness systems. This page covers all three.

Single hours and hour packs

  • The Single Hour ($100), Three-Hour Pack ($157), and Ten-Hour Pack ($379) are not refundable to cash once purchased — they're a bank of hours, not a reservation on their own.
  • Every hour in a pack must be used within 90 days of purchase. Unused hours expire after 90 days and aren't refunded or extended, except at our discretion for a documented emergency.
  • If you reserve a home wellness system within 90 days of buying a pack, whatever you paid for that pack is credited in full toward the system — this is the one case where a Lounge purchase converts directly into system credit.
  • Rescheduling a booked hour is free with at least 24 hours' notice. Cancelling with less notice, or not showing up, uses that hour from your pack.

Membership (Vita Recharge, Vita Restore, Vita Radiate)

  • Membership runs on a six-month minimum term. Monthly payments inside that term are not refunded for a month you didn't fully use.
  • You can cancel any time after the six-month minimum; cancellation stops the next billing cycle but doesn't refund the current one.
  • If we're unable to honor your membership hours for reasons on our side — the room closed for maintenance, for example — we'll credit the affected hours to your next billing cycle rather than issue a cash refund.

Home wellness systems (Advanced, Elite, Elite II)

  • Paid in full: fully refundable, minus a 5% processing fee, any time before your unit enters our production queue. Once it has entered production, it is built to your configuration and is non-refundable; we will work with you on store credit or a configuration change where possible.
  • Reserve with half: the 50% deposit is fully refundable if you cancel before production begins. The remaining balance, split into three equal monthly instalments, is due on the schedule confirmed at reservation; a payment more than 10 days late may move your position back in the delivery order.
  • Delivery dates are estimates. A delay on our side does not itself create a right to a refund, but it does extend any deadline tied to that delivery (for example, a trial-credit window) by the same number of days.

How to request one

Email hello@radiantwavelounge.com with your name and the email or phone number used at checkout. We'll confirm what applies to your purchase and, where a refund is due, process it back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Changes

We may update this policy as our offers change. The date at the top always reflects the version that applies to purchases made from that date forward; a purchase already made is covered by the policy in effect on the day you bought it.
