Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Radiant Wave Lounge ("we," "us") operates this site, the Lounge in Richmond, Virginia, and the home wellness systems we distribute. This page explains what we collect when you use this site, why, and what happens to it.

What this site collects

This site is a short questionnaire that sorts you toward the Lounge visit, membership, or home system most likely to fit you. As you answer, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number, if you give them to us.
  • Your answers — which options you pick, any room dimensions you enter, and which offer you land on.
  • Basic technical details: what referred you here and any campaign tags in the link you followed (UTM parameters).

We do not collect payment details on this site. Purchases and deposits are completed on a separate, secure GoHighLevel checkout page, which has its own receipt and its own handling of your card or bank details — this site never sees them.

Where it's stored

Your answers are held in your browser's session storage as you go — not a tracking cookie, and not visible to anyone but you until you submit. If you close the tab without finishing, that record is gone. If you submit a form, your answers and contact details are sent once, directly, to our customer system (a GoHighLevel CRM) so our Richmond team can follow up. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with other companies for their own marketing.

Why we collect it

  • To reach you about the hours, membership, or system you asked about.
  • To open your details pre-filled on the checkout or calendar page you're sent to, so you don't have to type them twice.
  • To understand which questions and offers are working, in aggregate — never to single out one visitor's answers for anything but following up with them.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. If we run ads on other platforms (for example Meta or Google), those platforms may place their own cookie or pixel when you arrive from an ad; that is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one.

Your choices

You can use this site without giving your name or contact details — you simply won't be able to submit a form or reach a checkout page that needs them. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email us at hello@radiantwavelounge.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time and confirm once it's done.

Children

This site and the Lounge are intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the current version.
